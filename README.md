# Lightkeeper

A story-driven, retro-radar survival game. You run the lighthouse. Every ship that comes near wants something, and you decide who lives, who docks, and who is turned away.

Play at [link](https://sumanto.itch.io/lightkeeper)

## THE JOB
Ships appear on your radar and offer trades. Guide them with your light:

White light attracts
Red light repels
Some ships do not obey.
Dock a ship to accept its trade. Keep it away to decline. Just make sure nothing crashes because of you. Wrecks hurt the harbour.

Sounds like a routine job?



## THE PLAGUE
A sickness is spreading through the seas. Infected ships wear happy masks.
Listen to the radio. Read between the lines. Not everyone who smiles is safe.

Stay safe. Stay alive. Keep your resources in check, and only make contracts you can fulfil.





##C ONTROLS
Left / Right	  Turn the light
Up / Down	      Light strength (reach)
Space	          Switch white / red

The knob, slider and button also work with the mouse.



## SURVIVAL NOTES
Food drains steadily. At zero, it drains your health rapidly.
Plague builds up and slowly drains health too.
Ships offer money, food and medicine. Some bring infection.
Crashes damage the harbour. Only dock what you can afford.
